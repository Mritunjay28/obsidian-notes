In interview we may be ask to
` Estimate your system capacity OR  Estimate your Performance Requirement Using Back-Of-The-Envelope-Estimation`

this Back-Of-The-Envelope-Estimation is combination of Estimates + Thought Experiment + Common Preference number Which design will meet our requirement .

Some Concepts to be aware of : 
1. Power of 2 : for 2^10 =1024 , approx = 1000 
   ![[Pasted image 20260709171132.png|Pasted image 20260709171132.png]]
2. Latency number
   Something to observe : 
	   1. Memory is faster than disk .
	   2. Avoid disk seeks if possible .
	   3. simple compression algo are fast  .
	   4. compress data before sending if possible .
	   5. data center are far located so time taking . 
   ![[Pasted image 20260709171241.png|Pasted image 20260709171241.png]]
3. Availability number  :
	   It's a number which shows that for how long our service will be available for / uptime / operation available time 
	   High Availability mean System continually operation for long time .
	   if 100% then 0 down time . Generally 99% standard .
	   ![[Pasted image 20260709172233.png|Pasted image 20260709172233.png]]
	   
	   We have SLA(Servic Level Agreement) :
	   Agrement btw service provider and customer for how much uptime availability
		eg : Amazon ,google
